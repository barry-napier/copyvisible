It reads your screen.
So it never leaves your Mac.

Who reads the text?
Apple’s Vision framework, on your Mac. Smart actions use Apple’s on-device model.
Does it use the internet?
Only to check for updates and download them. No account, no tracking.
When does it look at the screen?
Only when you press the shortcut, and only at the part you choose. macOS calls this permission Screen Recording. It never records video.
What does it save?
History, on your Mac only. You choose how long to keep it, or switch it off. Delete all really deletes it.
Can it get things wrong?
Yes. Reading text from a picture is not perfect, and neither is AI. Check before you send.
Can I check all this?
Yes. The code is open, under the MIT License.

Stop retyping what’s on your screen.

  2. Open CopyVisible.dmg, then drag CopyVisible into Applications.
